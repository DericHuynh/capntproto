# Code of conduct

Capn't Proto welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone
participating in the project is expected to help make its spaces respectful,
accessible, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with courtesy and respect across differences in identity,
  nationality, disability, age, religion, experience, and personal circumstances.
- Discuss technical decisions using evidence. Critique work and ideas without
  attacking the people behind them.
- Welcome questions, give constructive feedback, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect boundaries, requests to stop contact, and other people's privacy.
- Make room for others to participate and follow reasonable moderation requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, intimidation, sexual harassment,
unwanted sexual content, and personal attacks are unacceptable. This includes
publishing another person's private information without permission, repeated
unwanted contact, retaliating against someone for reporting a concern, and
deliberately disrupting project discussions.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, other
project-managed spaces, and interactions in which someone represents Capn't Proto.
It applies to contributors and maintainers alike.

## Report a concern

Email [huynhderic@gmail.com](mailto:huynhderic@gmail.com) with the subject
`Capn't Proto conduct report`. Include what happened, where and when it happened, and
links or screenshots if safe to share. Describe any immediate concern and the
best way to contact you. You do not need to confront the person involved or post
a report publicly.

Reports will be handled privately, with details shared only as needed to assess
the concern and take action. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised when
safety or platform intervention requires disclosure. If reporting to the project
maintainer is not appropriate, GitHub offers a separate
[abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
This is a platform reporting option, not an independent Capn't Proto appeals team.

## Enforcement and review

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or close
a discussion to protect participants. Depending on severity and repeated
behavior, a response may include a request to correct conduct, a warning, a
temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment can
justify immediate restrictions.

When appropriate, maintainers will explain the behavior at issue, the action
taken, and any conditions for returning. A person affected by a decision may
request reconsideration through the same private contact, with relevant context
or new information. Retaliation against reporters or participants is itself a
violation of this policy.
